Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a flight?

Simply search for available flights using your origin, destination, and travel date. Select your preferred flight and complete the booking with your account.

How can I cancel or modify my booking?

You can cancel or modify your booking from your profile page under "My Bookings". Cancellations made 24 hours before departure are fully refundable.

How do I track my flight status?

Go to the Flight Tracker section, enter your flight number or route, and get real-time updates on departure, arrival, and delays.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click on "Forget Password" on the login page, enter your username, and follow the steps to reset your password securely.

Is my personal data safe?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal information and payment data. Your privacy is our top priority.
